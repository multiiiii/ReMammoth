ReMammoth

Privacy Policy — last updated June 2026

Overview

ReMammoth is an offline-first personal memory app. All data you enter — notes, friend information, and birthdays — is stored exclusively on your device using a local SQLite database. The app does not require an account and does not connect to the internet.

Data we collect

We collect no data whatsoever. ReMammoth does not transmit, upload, or share any information with the developer or any third party. There are no analytics, no crash reporters, and no advertising SDKs in the app.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Personal notes (title and body text)
  • Names and optional birthdays of people you add
  • Notes associated with those people

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the built-in backup feature, which saves a file to your own device that you can share wherever you choose.

Third-party services

ReMammoth uses no third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that could collect data about you.

Data deletion

You can delete all data at any time by uninstalling the app. Individual notes and contacts can be deleted from within the app.

Children

Because no data is collected or transmitted, ReMammoth is safe for users of all ages.
